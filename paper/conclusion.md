# Conclusion

We have presented Lumint, a unified multimodal fraud intelligence framework for digital payments. By combining specialized analysis engines for URLs, documents, and screenshot forensics with an explainable cross-modal fusion layer, Lumint addresses the gaps left by traditional unimodal systems. 

Experimental results on synthetic benchmarks demonstrate that our framework achieves high detection rate without causing excessive latency. Additionally, our anonymization utilities and evaluation layer provide a secure and reproducible workspace for conducting fraud research.

Future research will focus on integrating graph neural network (GNN) models into Fraud DNA and deploying the framework within high-throughput payment gateways to evaluate real-time performance.
