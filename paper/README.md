# Lumint: A Unified Multimodal Fraud Intelligence Framework with Explainable Risk Fusion for Digital Payment Fraud

This directory contains the paper draft scaffold, figures, tables, and references for the Lumint academic paper.

> [!WARNING]
> All results presented in this scaffold or generated by the synthetic benchmark suite are based on **synthetic fixture datasets** and do not represent final real-world evaluation results. Real-world dataset ingestion and evaluation are pending.

## Directory Structure

- `README.md`: Project overview and directory map.
- `outline.md`: High-level paper outline.
- `abstract.md`: Executive summary/abstract.
- `introduction.md`: Background, problem statement, and contributions.
- `related_work.md`: Literature review (TODO placeholders for final citations).
- `methodology.md`: Mathematical formulation of explainable fusion and risk metrics.
- `system_architecture.md`: Multimodal pipeline design (DocShield, PhishShield, Fraud DNA, UPI Shield).
- `evaluation.md`: Benchmark configurations and experimental setup.
- `results.md`: Current empirical results (synthetic benchmarks).
- `limitations.md`: Scope limits and threat model boundaries.
- `ethics.md`: Anonymization, privacy constraints, and dual-use considerations.
- `conclusion.md`: Key takeaways and future work.
- `tables/`: Latex/CSV format experimental tables.
- `figures/`: Diagrams and visualization assets.
- `references/`: Reference database (references.md).
